
## This is the operator's side of a sealed node

This node has different owners from its parent, so its own material lives in its `home` (see
`node.md`), under its own rules. This folder holds only the operator's view: notes, status and
decisions as the operator sees them.

- Read anything in the home. Write nothing from here into it (axiom 6).
- Changes to the home go through its own change process, with its owners' consent (axiom 12).
- Workers that change the home run inside it, with a self-contained brief (axiom 9).
