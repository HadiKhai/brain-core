# Brain dashboard: identity note (Approved)

**One line:** a calm control room for your projects. It's alive, and you can see at a glance what's moving, what's waiting on you, and what the Brain has learned.

**For whom, and the job:** the operator, several times a day, for a few minutes:
1. what needs me now;
2. what moved since I last looked;
3. is anything stuck or burning (time, tokens, heat);
4. what do my projects know.
Everything else is one click deeper.

**Feel:** quiet by default and alive where it matters. Think a flight deck at night, not a spreadsheet: a dark-first, low-noise surface where colour appears only when something needs attention or is happening now.

**Palette:**
- **Base:** deep graphite and ink, dark-first, with a true light mode for daytime.
- **Accent:** one living colour, **ember** (warm amber-orange), for "needs you" and primary actions.
- **Activity:** **signal green** for live and running.
- **Data-viz neutrals:** two greys.
- **No other colour** without a meaning.

**Type and density:** Geist for UI and Geist Mono for ids, counts and times. Dense by default (rows of about 40–48 px), large numerals for KPIs, and one title per page.

**Signature components** (what makes it the Brain, not a generic admin):
1. **The tree:** Brain → projects → coordinator and threads, in the sidebar. Each node has a live dot (running, waiting, idle) and a count of what needs you. Collapsible; the current item is highlighted.
2. **The pulse strip:** a thin timeline across the top of Overview showing merges, failures, questions and releases from the last 24 hours, as glowing ticks.
3. **The brain graph:** projects, specialists and learnings as a force graph. Search makes matches glow, recent activity pulses softly, and a click opens the item.
4. **Meters:** compact gauges for the weekly Claude and Codex meters, machine load and heat, and verify queue depth.

**Overview layout (decision dashboard):**
- **Top row of KPIs:** needs-you count, merges today, verify queue, the meters.
- **Left:** the "Needs you" list (top 5, View all).
- **Right:** "Moving now" (running jobs with progress).
- **Below:** the pulse strip and a small graph preview.
- Never more than 2 screens.

**Voice:** short and plain, like the coordinators' best messages. Verb-first actions ("Answer", "Release", "Open thread"). No ids or paths unless you ask, and no jargon.

**Motion:** subtle and meaningful. Live dots breathe, new events slide into the pulse strip, the graph settles. Nothing animates without a reason; motion is reduced when the OS asks.

**Not this:**
- stacked boxes or a third surface level;
- a page title repeated four times;
- default scrollbars;
- cards 20% of the screen tall;
- text where a number or a bar would do;
- the same look as the operator's other products.
